# S11 — Post-review functional and visual fixes

## Context

A black-box simulator review against Android found two functional/visual issues. The owner also reported that the iOS app icon artwork occupies roughly a quarter of the available icon area, with the remainder rendered as white background.

Reproduce every issue in the simulator before changing implementation. Keep Android behavior and visuals unchanged unless the root cause is a shared source asset; if such a change is required, justify it and run the corresponding Android checks.

## 1. Doctor summary sharing

Reproduction:

1. Create or open an illness with a diagnosis and at least one temperature or medicine record.
2. Tap «Для врача». The preview is generated with the correct child and illness data.
3. Tap «Поделиться».

Actual: the preview dismisses and the illness screen returns, but the system share sheet never appears. Reproduced twice with waits of up to 10 seconds. Sharing an existing diaper event does open the system share sheet.

Expected: tapping «Поделиться» opens the system share sheet with the same doctor-summary text shown in the preview. Canceling and completing a share must not dismiss or damage the illness, and repeated attempts must work.

Fix the presentation flow rather than removing the preview or replacing it with a different user-visible workflow. Add UI coverage that proves the share sheet opens; retain the existing event-sharing behavior as a regression check.

## 2. Accessibility text layout

At `accessibility-extra-extra-extra-large`, the Today screen severely truncates the diary title, tile labels and bottom-tab labels, and the day summary extends behind the bottom navigation. At standard `extra-extra-extra-large`, the child subtitle already truncates part of the weight/height information.

Make the primary UI readable and operable with large accessibility text:

- the child identity and available measurements must remain readable;
- primary tiles and tabs must have understandable visible labels, with wrapping or another layout adaptation instead of ambiguous fragments;
- scrolling content must not be hidden behind bottom navigation;
- actions must remain reachable and usable without regressing standard text sizes or dark palettes.

Use simulator content-size changes and screenshot/UI tests for verification, not only a compile-time or static-layout check.

## 3. iOS app icon scale and white matte

The owner reports that the visible BabyLog artwork occupies about one quarter of the total icon area and the rest is white. Treat this as a defect in the rendered app icon, not as an intentional Apple safe-zone margin.

Inspect the actual Home Screen rendering and the source/generation path. Use the canonical `scripts/ios-icons.sh` generation path and source assets; do not patch generated files manually. Find why the artwork is reduced or surrounded by an unintended white matte and fix it at the source.

Acceptance criteria:

- the icon uses the intended BabyLog composition and scale at all required iOS sizes;
- there is no unintended white border, white matte or quarter-size artwork;
- transparency and Apple's icon requirements are handled correctly without masking the defect with a different background;
- the fix is verified on the simulator Home Screen after a clean install or another reliable way to avoid stale icon caching;
- generated assets and repository checks remain consistent.
